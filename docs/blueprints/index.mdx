## What they have in common

Every blueprint below is a **composition**, not a special case. The platform does not have
an "ML mode" or a "sovereignty mode" — it has components, links and sub-components, and
these are arrangements of them.

That matters for two reasons. You can start from one and change it, because nothing about
it is fixed. And everything the rest of this documentation describes — generation,
validation, scanning, evidence, compliance, backup, lifecycle — applies to all of them
without qualification.

## Starting from one

The fastest path is to describe what you want to your AI client and let it propose the
composition from the real catalog. Blueprints are useful as a shared vocabulary for that
conversation — "something like the private AI blueprint, but with our own retrieval corpus
and no public exposure" is a complete brief.

Day 2 — operate and prove

Day 2
AI cannot run operations by improvising. It can run them inside a framework.

The stack agent is deployed into your stack, reads the same metadata the build produced, and turns every declaration into something measurable. It is the interface between an AI and your runtime — and the reason an AI is allowed near it.

Visual explainerOperations is where systems are lost

One sentence — “upgrade the broker on staging” — and the six things that have to move together for it to be true.

↗

What it is​

One component, one API, scoped to one stack. It carries the tooling to reach every technology in the stack, and it knows the topology because it reads the graph rather than discovering it.

ProveValidation

Every declared relationship proven layer by layer, plus a query language that turns the graph into executable tests.

MeasureScanners

Scored scans for network policy, admission policy, Kubernetes hardening, RBAC, secrets, configuration posture and vulnerabilities.

AttestEvidence and drift

Hash-chained runs, explained deltas, finding memory, and a git-mirrored audit trail.

RecoverBackup and recovery

What is protected, what can be proven to hold data, which restore points are usable, and guarded restore.

ChangeLifecycle and scale

Upgrades and migrations from declarative recipes, autoscaling from real signals, restart-on-change, and workload queueing.

CertifyCompliance automation

Framework controls measured from the cluster, with a signed human position on every gap.

Why a separate agent​

Because a claim made by the thing that generated the system is not evidence.

The build says what should exist. The agent runs inside the cluster, with its own view, and answers what does. When the two disagree, the disagreement is the finding — and it is attributed: to a declared change you made, to an environment change, or to drift nobody asked for.

How you reach it​

Three ways, same engine:

ThroughFor
Your AI client, over MCPThe normal way. Ask a question, get an answer with evidence
The dashboardA visual surface for scans, scores, timelines, evidence export and audit-friendly printing
The HTTP APIAutomation and CI. Every scan, query and report is an endpoint

All three are optional surfaces over the same measurements, so a number shown in the dashboard, quoted by the model and exported for an auditor is the same number.

What it is deliberately not​

It is not a GitOps drift detector comparing a manifest to a live object. It compares the outcomes of executed checks over time, and explains each change. A control that passed last week and fails today is a finding with a first-seen date, an age, a reopen count and a classification — not a red line in a diff.

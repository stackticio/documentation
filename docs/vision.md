# 🚀 Introduction to Stacktic

Stacktic is a **new concept**, so we’ll begin with a short introduction explaining the **problems** in today’s ecosystem and the **idea behind Stacktic** as a solution.

---

## ❗ Problems

- **Hyperscalers and managed services** have become the default for building custom applications—mainly due to **lack of in-house skills**, a sense of **virtual insurance**, and long-term **commitment contracts**.  
  Meanwhile, highly skilled or well-funded companies often choose **open-source, Kubernetes-agnostic** solutions.

- **Managed services** lock customers in, limiting **cloud flexibility** and **portability**. You pay extra for infrastructure in exchange for a *promise* of faster time-to-market.

- Companies now face tough decisions:  
  - Whether to adopt **private, container-based** LLaMA deployments (cost-effective and controlled) or **fast-moving AI services** (OpenAI, Gemini, Claude).  
  - Whether to stick with managed services or migrate to **containers/Kubernetes-based solutions**.  
  - How to transition from **VMs** or **Cloud Foundry** to **pure Kubernetes**.

- The reason a **full-stack, pure, cloud-agnostic, open-source solution** is often considered “Plan B” is the **operational burden**: managing versioning, observability, data pipelines, databases, and messaging on top of open source is tough. Adding **security** concerns makes managed services tempting.

- At the root, **all this complexity is about relationships**—between services and databases, between microservices, data pipelines, RBAC, metrics, and observability. These are **stack relationships**.

## 💡 What Is Stacktic?

At its core, **Stacktic is a logic framework** designed to:  
- Integrate and **understand relationships** between services.  
- **Automate** those relationships—from application topology and ETL processes to Day-2 operations.

![alt text](image-10.png)

On top of the logic framework, Stacktic provides a **UI layer** to deliver a **modular platform engineering approach**:  
- Anything—inside or outside Kubernetes—can be **integrated, automated, or connected**.  
- Using metadata, Stacktic creates **real version control for every element of the stack** and its layers.

### 🔄 Stack Version Control
Stack version control enables previously impossible tasks:  
- **Migrate** to new services or versions out-of-the-box.  
- **Duplicate** stack versions for production, staging, QA, or testing.  
- **Rollback** an entire stack to a previous version.  
- **Redefine complexity as versioned, trackable changes**.

## 🌟 Our Value

- **Reduce Day-0 architecture and deployment planning** from months to **hours**.  
- **Cut deployment pain and costs** by **70% or more**.  
- **Eliminate Ops overhead** by automating SRE, DataOps, and SecOps—allowing engineers to focus on customization and improvement, not configuration.  
- **Automate security**, saving costs while removing release-time and production blockers.  
- **Cloud-agnostic stacks**: deploy with a single command on any cloud, saving **30–50% in infrastructure costs**, supporting **data regulations**, improving **negotiation power**, and **removing vendor lock-in**.  
- **Sovereignty beyond cost**: Stacktic enables **private ownership** and fully supports **sovereignty principles**.

## 🌟 Vision

Stacktic **bridges the gap** between the **disadvantages**—such as long time-to-market and operational overhead—and the **advantages** of pure open-source Kubernetes stacks.  
By bypassing skill gaps and reducing operational complexity, Stacktic delivers **fully stable, automated, cloud-agnostic, and sovereignty-compliant stacks**.

Our vision is to serve as a **unified platform engineering solution** that **democratizes open source**, boosts adoption, and **removes vendor lock-in**—providing full freedom to **innovate, adapt, and keep pace with technology**.

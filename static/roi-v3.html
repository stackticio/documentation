📊 Understanding the Calculation

💰 CAPEX (Capital Expenditure)

What is it? One-time costs to set up NEW applications

When? Year 1 only (setup phase)

Includes:

  • 📐 Day 0: Architecture & Design (4 tasks)
  • 🏗️ Day 1: Infrastructure Setup (13 tasks)

Applied to: 3 new apps

🔄 OPEX (Operating Expenditure)

What is it? Recurring costs to maintain EXISTING applications

When? Every year (ongoing operations)

Includes:

  • ⚙️ Day 2: Operations & Maintenance (9 tasks)
  • 🔁 Tasks run 1-12 times per year

Applied to: 10 existing apps

20 marks · text + icon

Twenty ways to make the tactic visible.

Every wordmark below does one job: quiet the S and the K so TACTIC surfaces. Every icon is monoline, one ink, and shown at 16px — because a mark that only works big is a decoration. Nothing here is added for a meaning; the meanings are found in geometry that had to be there anyway.

TAC is shared. STACK and TACTIC overlap on it, and the K is the letter STACK drives into TACTIC. Two words, one form, nothing invented — that's the entire brand argument, and it's already sitting in the name.

What the 16px row decides 05, 16 and 18 are out on execution, not concept — hairlines, ghosting and strikethroughs are the first things to die. 14 has no one-colour form. 19 explains the name better than anything else here and is still not a logo. Real shortlist Text: 01 (weight split) or 02 (case split). 02 is the sleeper — sTACkTIC needs no colour, no rules and no custom artwork, so it survives a plain-text email, a terminal and a partner's slide deck untouched.
Icon: 01 (stem + chevron + hub) or 04 (git-K). 01 is the smart-basic answer — three parts, and every one of them load-bearing. 04 says more and costs you legibility at 16px. The one open trade Whether the mark should name git explicitly (04) or stay at the level of graph + source of truth (01). 01 is truer to what Stacktic is — git is where it writes, not what it is.

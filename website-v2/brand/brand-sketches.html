Mark sketches · round 2

Git branches, and something more modern.

Same rule throughout: nothing is added for a meaning — the meanings have to be found in geometry that was going to be there anyway. Every sketch is monoline, one ink, and shown at 16px so you can see which ones actually survive.

Family AGit — branches, commits, merge

Git is the substrate Stacktic writes to. The question is whether that can be said inside a letterform, or whether it forces you out of one.

06Git-K

Trunk with commit nodes; the branch point sits mid-mainline and two branches diverge from it. Reads as a git graph and as a K at the same time — the only sketch here that manages both.

strongest new idea
07Merge loop

Branch leaves the mainline and always merges back. The cleanest possible statement of "divergence reconverges to one truth". But it isn't a K — it reads closer to a D, so it can't be a letterform lockup.

meaning wins, letter lost
08Tree-K

Branches subdivide twice — the most literal "tree". Look at the 16px: it turns to mush. It also reads organic, which is the wrong register for accurate, compliant, automated.

too busy · wrong register

Family BModern — reduction, negative space, versions

"Modern" here means fewer parts, not more effects. The test is how much you can remove before the meaning breaks.

09Two-stroke

A K is mathematically undrawable in one stroke — two is the floor. So this is the most reduced honest mark that exists: stem + chevron, nothing else. Holds stack, graph, chevron, K. Loses the source-of-truth vertex.

most reduced
10Two-stroke + hub

The same two strokes plus one dot. Three parts total, and the dot buys back the graph vertex and the source of truth. Open ends read as "connects onward" — an extensible graph, not a closed shape.

smart-basic candidate
11Check leg + hub

Adds validation: the leg overshoots into a tick. Says the most of any sketch — but check the 16px. The overshoot is the first thing to blur, and it makes the glyph noticeably wider than the others.

says most · widest
12Negative tile

Same K, knocked out of a gradient rounded square. Not a separate idea — it's the app-icon form of whichever mark wins. Worth having, but it can't be the primary: it dies in one colour and in print.

app icon only
13Version offset

The same K repeated and faded back — snapshots of the whole stack over time. A genuinely distinctive idea and true to the product. But it collapses into a smudge below 28px, so it's a motion/hero device, not a mark.

motion device, not a mark

CheckThe shortlist on #2A2847

Nothing is decided until it works reversed on the site background.

What the 16px row tells you. 08 and 13 are out — detail count, not concept, kills them. 07 is the best meaning and the worst fit: it stops being a K.

Real shortlist: 06, 10, 11. They differ on one axis — how much you're willing to carry at small size. 10 says stack + graph + truth in three parts. 11 adds validation and gets wider. 06 adds git and gets busier, but it's the only one where the mark names the substrate you actually write to.

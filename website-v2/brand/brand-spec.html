Brand plan · wordmark + type

The K is the stack.

Stacktic isn't stack glued to tactic — the two words overlap, and exactly one letter sits outside the overlap. That letter is the whole idea, and it happens to live dead centre of the name. This is the plan for the wordmark, the mark, and one type system to replace the three currently in flight.

01

The concept

The name splits into four parts, and they interlock: S · TAC · K · TIC. S + TAC + K spells STACK. TAC + TIC spells TACTIC. TAC is shared — and the K is the letter STACK drives into the middle of TACTIC.

S  TAC  K  TIC   ← stacktic S  TAC  K  TIC   ← stack S  TAC  K  TIC   ← tactic, interrupted TACTIC is whole inside the name — the K is what breaks it, and that break is the idea

So the wordmark has one job: quiet the S and the K until TACTIC surfaces. Twenty ways of doing that are drawn in brand-20-ideas.html.

Unification

Separate layers resolve into one silhouette. The stack stops being a pile and becomes a single form.

Automation

The same stroke read sideways is a chevron — flow, fast-forward, the separator in AI › STACKTIC › GIT › CLUSTER.

Validation

Angle the stroke and it's a tick. Compliance isn't a badge bolted on the side — it's the same geometry.

Graph

A K is three edges meeting at one vertex. It is already a graph — the nodes-and-links picture of the whole stack, drawn as a letter.

Source of truth

That vertex is a single point. AI, stacks, clusters and frameworks all route through it. One junction, one truth.

All five, two strokes

Layers · chevron · check are the same two strokes rotated. Add the vertex where they meet and you get graph + source of truth for free. That's why one glyph holds all five without becoming a collage.

02

The second reading: the K is the junction

Stacktic's actual substance is a directed graph over all the metadata of a stack — components, links, versions, policies. It is the source of truth that AI, stacks, clusters and frameworks all read from and write to. A letter K is the smallest possible drawing of that: three edges converging on one vertex.

One oversized node sits where the three strokes meet — the graph vertex. The four open ends are the things that connect to it. Nothing about this is decoration: it is a literal, accurate picture of what the product is.

Why Sora for the mark

Geometric with squared terminals — reads engineered, not soft. Already live on vision and the Wix one-pager, so half the surfaces need no change. Its K has a high, tight junction, which is exactly the anatomy the check-leg redraw needs. Free, variable, and it pairs cleanly with JetBrains Mono.

Migration

Three files swap one Google Fonts link and one --font token: home.html, product.html, solution.html. Body text moves to Sora; anything that should stay "terminal" (labels, evidence, the chain) moves to JetBrains Mono rather than staying Fira Code. Print one-pager keeps DM Sans — different medium, deliberate exception.

05

Colour rules

The logo is one ink. The gradient is an accent that lives on the mark and on rules and underlines — never inside letterforms.

Accentmark only

Do

  • Set the wordmark in a single ink — black, white, or #0e1426
  • Use the gradient on the mark, hero rules and underlines
  • Keep clear space of one mark-width on every side
  • Colour the K differently only in the animated web nav, never in exports

Don't

  • Run a gradient through the letters — it dies at 16px, in print, and one-colour
  • Hyphenate the name in the mark (S-Tactic)
  • Keep the curved A apex from the current logo — the K carries the idea now
  • Stack the mark above the wordmark; horizontal lockup only
  • Use the layer-stack mark and the K-monogram in the same layout

07

Open decisions

Mark variantB (check-leg K) is the recommendation — it's the only one whose meaning survives 16px. A and C are drawn here so the comparison is real, not described.

The K in running textDoes the drawn K appear only in the logo, or also in large display headlines where "Stacktic" is set? Logo only is safer; headline use is a distinctive move but needs the glyph to be genuinely optically matched first.

Existing layer-stack markRetire it, or keep it as a secondary "stack" icon in product diagrams? Recommendation: retire from brand use, keep the three-rhombi form as a diagram primitive only.

Final artworkThese are CSS/SVG approximations. Locking the mark means redrawing the K on Sora's actual outlines with hand-corrected junction weight and kerning against C and T.

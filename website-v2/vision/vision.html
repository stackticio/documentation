01
Our beliefs 01 · Open source

Not the cheap option. The better one.

Sovereignty is a consequence, not the reason. The reason is that open source is the stronger way to solve hard problems.

More advanced The best work in databases, streaming, identity and observability happens in the open first. Managed services package it later.
More flexible Every component replaceable, every version a choice. Nothing fixed because a vendor's roadmap says so.
More modular Hard problems get solved by composing parts, not by waiting for one product to grow a feature.
More automatable You cannot automate what you cannot see. Open components expose their behaviour, so the automation can be real.
and the same property makes it portable and cloud-agnostic — not tied to whoever hosts it this year
02
Our beliefs 02 · Unification

They connect in pairs. Never as a whole.

Strimzi runs Kafka. Debezium publishes through Kafka Connect. Grafana reads Prometheus. Those integrations are real — and each one stops at two.

What exists today
Working pairs, and nobody in charge of the set.
  • —Each pair wired by hand, by whoever knew both sides that week
  • —No central control — no one place that holds the whole
  • —No compatibility management — does this connector work with that version of that database? Nobody says
  • —No awareness — change one thing, find out about the third hop in production
The missing part of the ecosystem
Not more integrations. Metadata a machine can act on.
  • →What each component is, needs, and emits
  • →Which versions may face each other across a link
  • →Who may reach whom, and over what
  • →What each one proves — the control, and the evidence

Our beliefs 05 · The graph is the truth

One graph holds the system. Everything else is generated.

Not documentation someone remembers to update. A graph of every component, every link and every version — and the manifests, diagrams and evidence generated from it.

Where the truth lives today
Four descriptions of one system, kept apart by hand.
  • ·the running cluster — the only one that is actually true
  • ·the architecture diagram — drawn once, never revisited
  • ·the written documentation — correct on the day it was written
  • ·the audit evidence — reassembled from scratch every time

06
Our beliefs 06 · Governed AI

AI should be powerful. And governed.

Not trusted because it sounds confident — constrained, validated, and leaving a trail behind it.

What it reads The whole graph — every component, relationship and version. Not one folder of YAML.
What it may do Only what you allow, per environment. Read-only in production until you say otherwise.
What it must pass Validation. The rules the relationships carry have to hold, or the change does not exist.
What it leaves A reviewable diff in Git, with the evidence — so you can see exactly what it did.
Our unique approach

We don't integrate tools. We encode how they relate.

Every component carries machine-readable metadata, every relationship is a first-class link — and the whole system, operations included, is generated from that graph.

01 Encode Every component carries machine-readable metadata — what it is, what it needs, what it emits, what it proves.
02 Link Relationships are first-class. A link generates real configuration — routes, credentials, policies, monitoring.
03 Generate The entire system is generated from the graph — manifests, documentation, diagrams, evidence. Nothing written twice.
04 Operate Day-two work is calculated from the same graph — upgrades, migrations, compliance, scale. Operations, automated.
one graph in — a running, governed system out

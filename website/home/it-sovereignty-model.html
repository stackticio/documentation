M&A Carve-Out
When a company sells a division, the carved-out entity inherits the parent's IT — oversized, over-licensed, and tied to a TSA that burns cash every month. Stacktic isolates the relevant systems, data, and infrastructure from the parent organization and stands up a right-sized, independent IT environment.
Step 1
Identify & isolate
Map which systems, data, and users belong to the carved-out entity
Step 2
Deploy sovereign stack
Independent IT on the entity's own infrastructure — fully decoupled
Step 3
Migrate & right-size
Move only the relevant data. Right-size for 300 users, not 10,000
Step 4
Exit TSA
Independent operations. TSA terminated. IT cost drops. Valuation goes up.

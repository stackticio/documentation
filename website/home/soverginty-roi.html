Methodology & Data Sources Pricing: Default SaaS prices are based on publicly listed vendor pricing pages (March 2026). Where vendors don't publish pricing (e.g., ServiceNow), we use industry analyst estimates and note this explicitly. All prices are editable — adjust to match your actual contracts.
Feature Parity: Two scores per tool. Core = coverage of daily-use features (communication, data entry, reporting, admin) based on feature-by-feature comparison against vendor documentation. Full = coverage of the entire vendor ecosystem including marketplace, AI, niche modules. Scores are internal assessments, not third-party audits — treat as directional, not absolute.
Migration Hours: Estimates cover data migration effort only (Stacktic handles deployment and configuration). Based on typical mid-market implementations. Actual hours vary significantly by data volume, customizations, and integrations. Shown as single estimates — plan ±50% variance.
Infrastructure: Node costs based on GKE e2-standard-4 (4 vCPU, 16GB RAM) at ~$350/mo including persistent disks and network. Ops overhead set at 20% of infra cost, covering monitoring, patching, and incident response for a Stacktic-managed environment (vs. 40-60% for unmanaged self-hosting).
Ramp-Up: Year 1: 40%, Year 2: 75%, Year 3: 95% of tools migrated. Based on typical enterprise migration timelines where tools are migrated in phases, not all at once. Adjust expectations based on your team's capacity.

How Does Stacktic Save You Money?

Stacktic replaces your SaaS vendor subscriptions with open-source tools running on your own infrastructure. Here's the difference:

What You Pay Today
You pay SaaS vendors like Salesforce, Slack, and Google a per-employee monthly fee for each tool.
Example: Salesforce charges $80/user/month. For 150 CRM users, that's $144,000/year — just for CRM.
Hire 100 more people? Your SaaS bill goes up by $10K-$100K/year.
→
What You Pay With Stacktic
You pay for server infrastructure (Kubernetes cluster) + Stacktic license. That's it.
The same cluster runs CRM, Email, Chat, BI, and more. Adding more users costs almost nothing.
Hire 100 more people? Your infrastructure cost stays the same.
What does Stacktic actually do?
Stacktic is NOT just open-source tools. Without Stacktic, you'd need a team of DevOps engineers to install, configure, integrate, secure, update, and monitor every tool. Stacktic automates all of that: one-click deployment, shared SSO (single login for all tools), unified monitoring, automated security patches, backups, and Day 2 operations. You get the savings of self-hosting without the pain of managing it yourself.
